AltWing uses limited usage analytics to understand how people explore aerospace experiences and to improve the product. AltWing does not require an account and does not intentionally ask visitors for names, email addresses, schools, home addresses, or other identifying profile information.

What we measure

AltWing records product usage events that help us understand whether the experience is useful.

  • Page visits and navigation activity
  • WingMatch interactions and completion
  • Aerospace Wing and Lab exploration
  • Proof of Flight completion
  • Cosmic Pack and collection interactions
  • Basic browser and technical information associated with analytics events

What we do not require

  • No AltWing account
  • No name
  • No email address
  • No school name
  • No home address
  • No password or login profile

Why we use analytics

Usage data helps us understand where people stop, which aerospace experiences they explore, and whether they continue into deeper engineering activities. We use those patterns to improve AltWing and evaluate the experience in aggregate.

Analytics provider

AltWing currently uses PostHog for product and web analytics. Visitors are tracked using browser-generated identifiers rather than an AltWing login identity.

Session recording

Session replay is disabled in the AltWing client configuration. AltWing uses event-based analytics instead of recording and replaying visitors' on-screen sessions.

If AltWing changes

If AltWing later introduces accounts, collects new categories of information, or substantially changes how analytics work, this notice should be updated before those features are released.
